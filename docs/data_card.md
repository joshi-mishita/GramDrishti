# Data card

> **Every dataset GramDrishti uses today is synthetic.** The district, Panchayats, stations, forecasts, observations, crops and farmer reports were made by `data/generate_mock_data.py`. They do not describe any real place, station or person. No real data has been loaded, and the team has no data-sharing agreement with any organisation.

## Personal data

- The five demo farmers (`store/seed_demo.py`) are named "Demo farmer 1" to "Demo farmer 5". They are not people.
- A farmer record holds a name, Panchayat, language, crops with sowing dates and whether they keep livestock. **No phone number, address, Aadhaar number or location beyond the Panchayat is stored.**
- A feedback report holds Panchayat, date, yes/no, intensity, channel and a timestamp. It does not hold who sent it.
- The WhatsApp share button opens `https://wa.me/?text=...` with the advice text only; no number is asked for (DECISIONS D112).
- The API's access log (JSON in Docker) records method, route template, status, duration and data mode. It never records client addresses, user agents, request bodies, farmer ids or feedback (`api/hardening.py`, DECISIONS D125).
- There is no login. The reviewer name on the review screen is free text ("Demo officer" by default), written into the audit log.

## Known limits of the mock data

From [data/README.md](../data/README.md):
- The planted relationships are far simpler than real weather. A model that beats the block forecast here proves the code works, not that the method works on real data.
- Block forecasts are built from the block truth, so forecast errors are realistic in form but known and simple.
- Within-block Tmax spread is modest (about 0.1 to 0.8 C by season); rain is patchy.
- Two years exercise train and test splits but do not represent climate variability.
- Forecast errors are independent across lead days, and stations sit only in Panchayats (24 of 90).
- Mock elevations range from 210.0 to 218.3 m, so the lapse-rate term in B2 barely matters.
- Every Panchayat has soil texture "loam".
